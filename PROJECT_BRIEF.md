This project is an astro application designed to determine what would it take for an F1 driver in the current season to win the championship.
For example, we have the driver standings, what would it take for the 4th place driver currently to win the championship. It could be that we need the driver in the 1st place to not get a point in the next couple of races and the 4th place driver to win the next few grand prix. 

For the UI design of the F1. We have to follow the [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/) website.
